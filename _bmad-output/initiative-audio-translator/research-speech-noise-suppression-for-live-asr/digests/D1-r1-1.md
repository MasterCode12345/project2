### Cross-model note for the decision (synthesis, not a source claim)
- On the same test set (VoiceBank+DEMAND), self-reported causal PESQ is DFN3 3.17 > Demucs-causal 2.93 > GTCRN 2.87. Each figure comes from its own paper, so the comparison holds only loosely; training-data and protocol differences are not controlled.
- On cost, GTCRN (about 40 MMACs/s, 23.7K params) and RNNoise (about 40 MFLOPS, 87.5K weights) are orders of magnitude cheaper than Demucs, which needs RTF 0.6-1.05 on a laptop i5 and is marginal on a CPU shared with Whisper. DFN3 is in between (RTF 0.19 single-thread on an i5-8250U).
- Latency budgets: Demucs 40 ms frame / 16 ms stride; DFN3 40 ms; PercepNet 40 ms lookahead. RNNoise and GTCRN are 10 ms hop with small or no lookahead (RNNoise's ms figure is not in the paper).

## Leads worth chasing next round
- GTCRN full paper (ICASSP 2024) to confirm 23.7K params / 39.6 MMACs / PESQ 2.87 / DNSMOS 3.44, the RTF and the lookahead, and to resolve the 48.2K conflict.
- DeepFilterNet2 paper (IWAENC 2022, arXiv 2205.05474) and DFN v1 (ICASSP 2022, arXiv 2110.05588) for params and MACs (DFN2 is believed to be about 2.3M params and about 0.36 GMACs; unverified).
- NSNet2: Braun & Tashev, "Data augmentation and loss normalization for deep noise suppression" (SPECOM 2020, arXiv 2008.06412), for params, latency and DNS numbers.
- FRCRN (Zhao et al., ICASSP 2022, arXiv 2206.07293): params, MACs and the DNS/VoiceBank numbers; it is likely not low-complexity.
- ICASSP 2023 DNS Challenge (5th) summary paper and results, plus the newest lightweight SE papers from 2025-2026 (UL-UNAS, FSPEN, LiSenNet, and others), to make a fresh SOTA claim.
- noisereduce GitHub README (timsainb/noisereduce): which method it uses and what it cites (Audacity noise reduction / Sainburg et al. 2020 PLoS Comput Biol is believed; unverified).
- Classical canon: Boll 1979 (IEEE TASSP 27(2)), Ephraim & Malah 1984 (MMSE-STSA, IEEE TASSP 32(6)) and 1985 (log-MMSE, IEEE TASSP 33(2)), Scalart & Filho 1996 (decision-directed Wiener). These are known bibliographically but I did not retrieve them this run.
