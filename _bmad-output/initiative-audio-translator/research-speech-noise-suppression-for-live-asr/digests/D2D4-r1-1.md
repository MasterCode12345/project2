## What this means for the decision (inference, not a sourced claim)
- Every retrieved Whisper study (#7, #8, #9) shows a naive SE front-end hurting WER at moderate SNRs, and the artifact literature (#1–#5) explains why. Whisper is already robust down to about 10 dB (#6).
- The SE configurations most likely to be safe are: (a) no SE by default; (b) SE followed by observation adding with ω around 0.3–0.5 (#3); (c) SE gated by an estimate of SNR or DNSMOS (#10, #11).
- Any SE choice must be validated by WER on Vietnamese speech with noise added at controlled SNRs (for example 20, 10, 5, 0 dB). Perceptual metrics (PESQ, DNSMOS) did not predict ASR benefit (#8: PSNR went up while WER also went up).
