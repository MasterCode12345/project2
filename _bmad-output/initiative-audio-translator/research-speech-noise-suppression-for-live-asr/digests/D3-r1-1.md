## Implications for 16 kHz / 30 ms (480-sample) frames
- GTCRN and NSNet2 are native 16 kHz. GTCRN's 256-sample hop does not divide a 480-sample frame, so the pipeline needs a small ring buffer, which adds about 16–32 ms latency.
- RNNoise and DeepFilterNet need 16k→48k upsampling. One 30 ms frame becomes 1440 samples at 48k, which is 3 RNNoise frames or 3 DFN hops.
- WebRTC bindings use 10 ms frames: one 30 ms frame is 3 calls of 160 samples.
