## Synthesis for the decision (my inference, not a source claim)
- **Classical baseline set to cite:** Boll 1979 (spectral subtraction), then Ephraim-Malah 1984/1985 (MMSE-STSA and log-MMSE), then Scalart & Vieira Filho 1996 (decision-directed a priori SNR Wiener). All are causal, nearly free on CPU, and are the natural "classical" ablation arm.
- **noisereduce** is convenient, but its default non-stationary mode uses non-causal forward-backward smoothing and the library is batch-oriented. Live use would need the stationary mode over chunks, or a causal reimplementation. Its citable paper is a bioacoustics paper, not an SE paper.
- **NSNet2-style (Braun & Tashev 2020)** has 2.8M params, 16 ms hop and no lookahead, so it is a mid-weight learned baseline. GTCRN (48.2K counted fully, RTF 0.07 on i5-12400, MIT) remains the lightest learned option with official streaming code.
