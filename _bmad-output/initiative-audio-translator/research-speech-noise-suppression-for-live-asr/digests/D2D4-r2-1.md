## Answers to the round-2 questions
- **Q1 (Whisper with vs without DeepFilterNet, RNNoise or noisereduce, per SNR, and observation adding for Whisper):** No primary study was found this run. The only DeepFilterNet paired with Whisper was in a vendor blog and a product page, both without numbers, so they are excluded. Claim #12 is the closest lead. No Whisper-specific evidence on observation adding was found either; only the round-1 Kaldi/NTT results exist. This gap justifies the project's own experiment.
- **Q2 (metric and package sources):** SI-SDR (#4), STOI (#5), pystoi (#6), jiwer (#7) and TorchMetrics (#8) are covered. PESQ was covered in round 1 (`pesq` is MIT; ITU P.862 code licence unclear). The ITU-T P.862 and P.862.2 pages were not fetched.
- **Q3 (datasets):** FLEURS (#9), MUSAN (#10) and VoiceBank-DEMAND (#11, secondary only) are covered. DEMAND (Thiemann et al. 2013) and Common Voice Vietnamese were not retrieved.
- **Q4 (Whisper Vietnamese WER):** Fully answered by #1–#3.

## Leads
- Mawalim et al. Interspeech 2024 PDF: read Tables 1–3 for the ASR model and the WER of DeepFilterNet3 against noisy input. This needs a PDF text tool (pdftotext and pypdf are not installed here).
- arXiv 2608.20971 (DeepFilterNet3 trained with room-acoustic simulation reports lower ASR WER): check which ASR model it uses.
- FLEURS README from offset 100000 onward: find the size of the vi_vn split. FLEURS paper: arXiv 2205.12446 (my belief, not verified).
- Edinburgh DataShare record for VoiceBank-DEMAND (DOI 10.7488/ds/1356 is my belief, unverified), and the Thiemann et al. 2013 DEMAND Zenodo record.
- Common Voice Vietnamese: the Mozilla Data Collective / Common Voice dataset page (CC0 is my belief, unverified).
- ITU-T P.862 and P.862.2 recommendation pages at itu.int.
